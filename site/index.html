Six of the eight hundred, word for word.

Every question in the catalogue is written to be answered in a sentence, and answered privately, before anyone sees what anyone else said.

  • What small thing do I do that makes you feel cared for?
  • Which belief has changed most as you have grown?
  • What thought visits you when the world gets quiet?
  • Which shared memory still makes you laugh before you tell it?
  • What would I choose for a completely free afternoon?

Four steps, then you are deeper than you were.

  1. 01

    Create a room

    Pick a deck, put in a name, and you get a four character code like K3QF. Send it, or send the invite link, which opens DEEPER straight into the lobby on a phone that already has it.

  2. 02

    Answer in private

    The same question lands on every phone at once. You type your answer and nobody can read it, not even the person sitting next to you. That is the whole trick: an honest answer is only possible if it is sealed first.

  3. 03

    Reveal together

    Once everyone has answered, the room counts down from three and every phone reveals at the same instant. No scrolling ahead, no reading someone else's answer while you are still writing yours.

  4. 04

    React, then go deeper

    Tap love, thats funny, didnt expect that, or tell me more. Then the next question arrives. Ten questions per game, and the deck decides how hard they get.

The full rules, both game modes, and what happens when a connection drops

You do not answer. You predict.

In this deck each player writes down what they think the other person will say. At the reveal, your guess sits next to their real answer, and a point lands if the two match.

  • Scored across all ten questions, shown live as you play.
  • Matching is case and whitespace insensitive, so a near miss still counts as your real answer.
  • Two players only. Everyone else in the room is a spectator for this one.

Read what is inside each deck

Two to eight
Players in a room. Three decks are strictly two people, because their questions only work when there is exactly one other person answering.
Ten questions
Per game, then a scoreboard. Long enough to get somewhere, short enough to finish before the film does.
Four characters
In a room code. Type it, or send the invite link and skip typing entirely.

Your answers are not our product.

We built a game about saying the thing you meant. Keeping a copy of what you said would undo it, so the architecture does not do it.

Answers are never written to the database
There is no answers table. An answer lives in your phone's memory and in an encrypted room cache, and it is deleted the moment the game ends.
Encrypted at rest, and gone after the game
The cache is AES-256-GCM encrypted and expires after two hours whether or not you finish. No answer text is ever logged or sent to analytics.
No advertising identifier, ever
The app generates a random string on your phone and uses it as an account handle. It reads no IDFA, no Android Advertising ID, no IMEI, no MAC address.
No third-party trackers in the app
One purchase SDK, for the store. No ad network, no analytics vendor, no crash reporter wired up.
