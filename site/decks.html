How to play Decks Questions Privacy

Eight decks, one hundred and sixty base prompts, one tone each.

Each deck has 20 hand-written prompts. The catalogue expands every prompt with five suffix phrases, giving 100 question IDs per deck and 800 in total. The questions below are the base prompts, quoted verbatim from packages/content/src/index.ts.

Eight decks
Five unlocked, three behind an entitlement check that was never connected to a store. Three decks are strictly for two people, and one is age gated.
A hundred IDs each
A game draws ten at random. The selector avoids IDs used by the last ten games of that deck, but it assumes 100 distinct questions where there are really 20 prompts written five ways.
One hundred and sixty prompts
The headline number of 800 counts suffix variants, not distinct questions. It is a known inflation, documented in the README and the postmortem.
  • Couples

    Make the familiar feel new again.

    • What small thing do I do that makes you feel cared for?
    • Which ordinary moment with me would you happily replay?
    • What do you hope never becomes ordinary between us?

    100 question IDs · exactly 2 players · unlocked

  • Deep Conversations

    The questions beneath the questions.

    • Which belief has changed most as you have grown?
    • What does being understood feel like in your body?
    • What would you like to forgive yourself for?

    100 question IDs · 2 to 8 players · unlocked

  • How Well Do You Know Me?

    Predict, reveal, and compare. The only deck with a score.

    • What would I choose for a completely free afternoon?
    • Which tiny inconvenience tests my patience?
    • Which room in a house would I redesign first?

    100 question IDs · exactly 2 players · unlocked

  • Funny & Random

    Low stakes, high smiles. The one to open with.

    • Which animal would be the worst roommate for me?
    • What would I be arrested for in a cartoon universe?
    • Which household chore deserves an awards ceremony?

    100 question IDs · 2 to 8 players · unlocked

  • Friends

    Good friends. Better stories. Built for a group.

    • Which shared memory still makes you laugh before you tell it?
    • What is one way I make a room better?
    • Which inside joke needs to be retired but never will be?

    100 question IDs · 2 to 8 players · unlocked

  • First Dategated

    Skip the small talk. Warm, unhurried, and safe to answer honestly on a first meeting.

    • What is a green flag you notice quickly?
    • What is a question you wish dates asked more often?
    • Which ordinary pleasure never gets old?

    100 question IDs · exactly 2 players · gated in code

  • Midnight Conversationsgated

    For the talks that run late. The deck for a group that has already stopped performing.

    • What thought visits you when the world gets quiet?
    • Where do you feel most unobserved?
    • Which truth became easier after midnight?

    100 question IDs · 2 to 8 players · gated in code

  • After Dark18+

    A playful, age-gated deck about attraction, tension, and what makes a moment memorable.

    • What kind of chemistry do you notice first?
    • What makes a private moment feel memorable?
    • What boundary helps you feel more playful?

    100 question IDs · 2 to 8 players · gated in code · age gated

Start light. It is not a personality test.

Funny & Random is the safest opener for a group that has never done this, because nobody has to be vulnerable to get started. Deep Conversations and Midnight Conversations land much harder once the room already trusts each other.
